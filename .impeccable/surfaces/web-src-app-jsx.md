Scope: the whole ReSauce researcher workstation (web/): Cases, New case, Case workspace, Review queue, Audit trail, Export, Validation (secondary), About & limitations. Operate mode; About is Read. Audience: a researcher on their own computer for long review sessions; also shown on a projector. Constraint: every number and claim from the pipeline, the workspace files, the results files or the docs; synthetic data only.

## Direction contract
THESIS: every state is a sign. Like hospital wayfinding, each status is a fixed symbol + word + at most one colour, so the reviewer always knows where they are and what needs them. Refuses the KPI dashboard and the paper/editorial costume.
OWN-WORLD: deep petrol sign-panel sidebar (#0f3a44) with white legends; calm cool-neutral ground, white work surfaces; one teal accent reserved for actions and selection; one signal orange-red for discrepancy, always with ◆ and the word. Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for UI and notes, Plex Mono for IDs, dates, offsets. Hierarchy by type scale and a 68ch reading measure, not boxes (raise: specimen). One fixed severity ramp everywhere (raise: atlas). One focus at a time in the queue (raise: wall). Accent only on actionable things (raise: warm). Identical Day 0..+28 scale for every case (raise: folio); outside-window items shown as outside (raise: j-card).
STORY: bring cases, verify with the frozen pipeline, follow each finding to its quote, decide with a note, export with the pipeline version.
FIRST VIEWPORT: sidebar left; synthetic-data banner top; case header with ID, facts, baseline toggle and the single primary "Verify"; documents left (tabs with moving underline, note on a 68ch measure), record + finding sign-plates + timeline right.
