Scope: the whole ReSauce researcher workstation (web/): Pipeline (home), Import sheet, Case workspace, New case, Review, Audit, Export, Results (secondary), About. Operate mode, with an Experience moment on the Pipeline stage; About is Read. Audience: a researcher on their own computer for long review sessions; also shown on a projector. Constraint: every number and claim from the pipeline, the workspace files, the results files or the docs; synthetic data only.

## Direction contract
THESIS: the pipeline is the application. Each imported case is a mark that travels through the four real stages and resolves into one of three destinations; the motion reports real events, paced for viewing. Refuses the KPI dashboard, card grids and invented animation. Replaces the earlier "hospital signpost" world (user decision, 2026-11-09).
OWN-WORLD: jet black and pure white, bold system type (apple-design skill: platform font, size-specific tracking), strong 2px rules; a full-bleed black stage; four accents with one meaning each (red discrepancy, amber needs review, green no discrepancy detected, blue focus and selection), always with a drawn shape and a word. Plex Mono for IDs, dates, offsets. Numbers hidden until asked for (Numbers toggle, hover, inspector).
STORY: import synthetic cases, verify, watch each record resolve, open the inspector on any mark, follow a finding to its quote, decide in Review, export with the pipeline version.
FIRST VIEWPORT: translucent black top bar (logo, Pipeline · Review · Audit · Results · About, Import data); synthetic-data line; compact hero (statement left, lede and the one primary action right); the black stage with status in words, filters, Numbers and Network/List.
SIGNATURE: the run: signals leave their marks, light each stage, and branch into a red network under a turning beacon, an amber network, or converge into a plug that seats in its socket.
FINISH: polished with impeccable (polish pass, detector: one intentional advisory, the landing spring); DESIGN.md rewritten for this world; the design.json sidecar still describes the old world and should be regenerated with `$impeccable document`.
